Help

How do I print the labels?

Add your games, press Download PDF and open the file. In the print window choose Actual size or 100%. Never “Fit to page”. Each label is 63 × 16 mm.

How do I know the size is right?

Every page has a ruler at the bottom. Measure it: it must be exactly 50 mm. If it isn't, your printer scaled the page. Turn off scaling and print again.

What paper should I use?

Print one page on plain paper first and hold a label against a cartridge. When it fits, use matte sticker paper (A4 or US Letter) and cut along the small corner marks.

A label looks wrong. What can I do?

Most labels were made automatically from box art, so a few crops are off. Use Report under the label and say what's wrong. Fixes show up here automatically.
