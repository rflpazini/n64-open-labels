End labels for your N64 shelf

Free, printable top labels in one consistent style. Pick the games you own, build a sheet at actual size (63 × 16 mm) and print it at home.

Labels marked draft were generated automatically and haven't been hand-reviewed yet. If one looks off, use Report on its card.

How to print

  1. Pick your games with Add to sheet, or download a whole letter's PDF from its heading.
  2. Download the PDF from the tray at the bottom. Choose A4 or US Letter.
  3. Print at 100% / actual size. Turn off "fit to page". The 50 mm ruler on the page must measure exactly 50 mm.
  4. Test on plain paper first against a real cart, then print on sticker paper and cut along the corner marks.

Single PNGs are 300 DPI (600 DPI for the hand-tuned set). Print the PDF when you can; it keeps the exact size.
